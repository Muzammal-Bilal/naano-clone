The problem

BlogSEO was buying paid social and could see spend go out, but could not say which of it produced a trial. Attribution stopped at the channel, so every decision about where to spend more was a guess dressed up as a report.

What they did

They shortlisted creators by audience fit rather than reach, briefed all nine from the same campaign brief so the messaging stayed consistent, and gave every creator a tracked link tied to their booking.

The result

2,940 qualified clicks and 512 trials, each attributable to the specific post that produced it. The two best creators were not the two largest, which is the finding that changed how they spend.
